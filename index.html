About

I like problems where biology is messy, the data is high-dimensional, and the answer requires more than running a standard workflow.

I am a computational biologist with an M.S. in Bioinformatics from Johns Hopkins University. My work spans NGS and immune profiling, statistical modeling, scientific software, structural bioinformatics, molecular dynamics, and reproducible computing across Linux, HPC, and cloud systems.

I currently work at the Johns Hopkins School of Medicine in the Larman Lab, where I lead computational analysis for a colorectal-cancer PhIP-Seq study and help build the computational infrastructure for InFlux, a highly multiplexed sequencing-based influenza neutralization assay.

Selected work

Projects built to survive contact with real data.

A few projects that capture the range of my work, from immunology and virology to structural modeling and research software engineering.

01 / IMMUNOLOGY

CRC antibody-signature discovery with PhIP-Seq

Computational analysis across colorectal-cancer and control cohorts, including QC, groupwise statistics, prevalence-aware filtering, replication, and interpretable stability-selection modeling for sparse antibody-reactivity signatures.

PhIP-SeqRStatisticsMachine learning
02 / VIROLOGY

InFlux neutralization pipeline

Snakemake/SLURM workflow for a multiplexed influenza A neutralization assay, covering barcode-aware read processing, QC, molecular equivalence, titration fitting, and percent neutralization.

PythonSnakemakeSLURM
↗
03 / STRUCTURE

EpitopeFindeR 2 and structure-aware epitope mapping

Peptide-to-structure analysis using sequence alignment and FoldDisco-based structural mapping to identify continuous and discontinuous epitopes and localize antibody binding sites on antigen structures.

Structural bioinformaticsFoldDiscoSequence analysis
04 / SOFTWARE

drydock: reproducible high-throughput virtual screening

A Python CLI and GUI for receptor and ligand preparation, multi-engine docking, resumable screening, reproducible manifests, and practical failure handling for Linux workflows.
